# 13 · Call-to-Action Strategy

| Funnel stage | Students & graduates | Businesses | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Follow for daily tech tips / Save this | Follow for business growth tips | Reels, carousels, end screens |
| Consideration | Comment 'ROADMAP' / Take the track quiz | Download the website checklist | Carousels, polls, comment-to-DM |
| Lead capture | Get the free Fresher Project Kit | Get a free website health check | Link in bio, pinned posts, LinkedIn featured |
| Conversion | Apply free -> choose your track | Request a quote / Book a call | Website hero, sticky mobile button, DM replies |
| Advocacy | Share your task with #VedaTechnologyIntern | Leave a Google review | Post-completion emails, certificate page |

## CTA rules

- One primary CTA per post - never stack 'apply, follow, DM and share' together.
- Follow the 80/20 rule: about 80% of posts lead with value (save/share/comment) and 20% ask for an application or enquiry.
- Action verb + benefit + low friction: 'Apply free - takes 3 minutes' beats 'Click here'.
- Brand-consistent buttons: orange CTA on a navy background on website and creatives.
- Every CTA link carries UTM parameters so its performance can be measured.
