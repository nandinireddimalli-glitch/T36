## Audience segments

| Segment | Priority | Needs & motivations | Where they are online |
|---|---|---|---|
| Students (B.Tech/BCA/MCA/diploma, 18-22) | Primary - largest volume | Want a first internship and a credible certificate for their resume; afraid of fake or paid internships; need guidance on which skill to pick. | Instagram, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, college WhatsApp groups |
| Graduates & freshers (21-25) | Primary - highest intent | Job-hunting; need portfolio projects, an LOR and proof of practical skill to stand out to recruiters. | LinkedIn, Google Search, Naukri/Indeed, Telegram groups |
| Small & local businesses (owners 30-55) | Secondary - revenue | Need an affordable website, social presence and Google visibility; limited time and technical knowledge. | Facebook, Instagram, Google Search/Maps, WhatsApp |
| Startups & SMEs (founders/managers 25-45) | Secondary - revenue | Need app development, cloud setup or data/dashboard work delivered quickly by a reliable partner. | LinkedIn, Google Search |
| Influencers: college TPOs, faculty, parents | Supporting | Want safe, legitimate, free opportunities for students; care about verification and transparency. | LinkedIn, email, WhatsApp |

## Key insights

- Trust is the #1 barrier: students have seen many 'pay-to-intern' schemes, so verification IDs, MSME registration, mentor reviews and real intern work must appear in content constantly.
- Every intern already posts their work on LinkedIn as part of task submission - this is a free, built-in word-of-mouth engine that only needs a common hashtag and a template.
- Students consume short, visual, useful content (reels, carousels); businesses respond to plain-language problem/solution posts and free audits.
- Peak student intent windows: semester breaks (Dec-Jan, May-July) and placement season; business intent rises before festive and new-financial-year periods.
