# 02 · Target Audience Personas

## Priya - The First-Internship Student

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Segment | Student |
| Profile | 20, 2nd-year B.Tech (CSE), Tier-2 city college, limited budget, studies on her phone. |
| Goals | Get her first internship and certificate before 3rd year; build 2-3 projects for her resume. |
| Pain points | Doesn't know which track to choose; worried about fake/paid internships; no one to review her code. |
| Channels | Instagram reels, YouTube Shorts, college WhatsApp groups, LinkedIn (new user). |
| Key message | Free, virtual, beginner-friendly Level 1 tasks with mentor review and a verifiable certificate. |
| Primary CTA | Apply free -> choose your track |

## Arjun - The Job-Ready Fresher

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Segment | Graduate |
| Profile | 23, BCA/MCA graduate, applying to entry-level developer/analyst jobs, active on LinkedIn. |
| Goals | Show recruiters real, working projects and an LOR; move from 'certificates' to 'portfolio'. |
| Pain points | Gets rejected for 'no experience'; certificates without projects are ignored. |
| Channels | LinkedIn, Google search, Naukri/Indeed, Telegram job groups. |
| Key message | Level 2-3 tasks produce portfolio projects; performance-based LOR; certificates prove output. |
| Primary CTA | Start at Level 2 -> build your proof |

## Ramesh - The Local Business Owner

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Segment | Business |
| Profile | 42, runs a coaching centre/retail shop; uses WhatsApp and Facebook daily; no website. |
| Goals | More local enquiries; look professional online; appear on Google Maps. |
| Pain points | Thinks websites are expensive and complicated; burned by an unreliable freelancer before. |
| Channels | Facebook, Instagram, Google Search/Maps, WhatsApp. |
| Key message | Simple, affordable website + digital marketing from a registered company, explained in plain language. |
| Primary CTA | Get a free website health check |

## Sneha - The Startup / SME Decision-Maker

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Segment | Service customer |
| Profile | 31, operations head at a growing startup; needs an app, cloud setup or data dashboard. |
| Goals | Reliable delivery, clear scope and timeline, reasonable cost. |
| Pain points | Large agencies are costly; small vendors miss deadlines; hard to judge technical quality. |
| Channels | LinkedIn, Google Search, referrals. |
| Key message | One team for web, app, cloud and data work, with a transparent process. |
| Primary CTA | Request a quote -> share your requirement |
