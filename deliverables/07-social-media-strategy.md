# 07 · Social Media Strategy

**Objective:** build trust and reach with value-first content, then convert attention into applications and enquiries.

## Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | What it covers | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learn | 27% | Bite-sized tutorials, roadmaps, tool tips per track | Value / saves |
| Proof | 23% | Intern project showcases, mentor feedback, spotlights | Trust / social proof |
| Career | 20% | Resume, LinkedIn, interview and internship-safety guidance | Value / shares |
| Business | 17% | Website, app, cloud and marketing tips for business owners | Service leads |
| Brand | 13% | Batch announcements, milestones, behind the scenes | Applications |

## Platform roles

| Platform | Role | Main audience | Frequency | Formats |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Credibility hub | Graduates, TPOs, startups/SMEs | 4-5 posts/week | Carousels, text+image, polls, intern spotlights, articles |
| Instagram | Student reach & discovery | Students (18-22) | 5 posts/week + daily stories | Reels, carousels, stories, highlights |
| Facebook | Local business reach | Small business owners | 3 posts/week | Tip posts, checklists, local group sharing |
| WhatsApp / Telegram (optional) | Retention & announcements | Applicants, interns, campus groups | 2 updates/week | Batch dates, new tracks, webinar reminders |

## Brand & tone

- Navy (#0B1E4D) backgrounds, orange (#F97316) highlights and CTAs, one Canva template per format.
- Friendly-mentor voice: clear, honest, encouraging; no hype or unverifiable numbers.
- Real intern work (with permission) over stock images.
