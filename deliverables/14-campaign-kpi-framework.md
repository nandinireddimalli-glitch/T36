# 14 · Campaign KPI Framework

| Stage | KPI | How it is calculated | Tool | 30-day target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Total reach / impressions | Sum of unique accounts reached across platforms | LinkedIn & Meta Insights | 40,000+ reach |
| Awareness | Follower growth | (New followers / starting followers) x 100 | Platform insights | +15-25% |
| Engagement | Engagement rate | (Likes + comments + shares + saves) / reach x 100 | Platform insights, Google Sheets | LinkedIn 4%+, Instagram 5%+ |
| Engagement | Saves & shares | Count per post | Instagram/LinkedIn insights | Saves at least 3% of reach on Learn posts |
| Traffic | Website visits from social | Sessions with utm_medium=organic_social | Google Analytics 4 (concept) | 600+ sessions |
| Traffic | Click-through rate (CTR) | Link clicks / reach x 100 | UTM + platform insights | 1.0-1.5% |
| SEO | Search impressions & clicks | Impressions, clicks and avg. position for target keywords | Google Search Console (concept) | Impressions +30% vs. baseline |
| Conversion | Internship applications | Apply-form submissions attributed by UTM | Apply form + Google Sheets | 40-60 |
| Conversion | Lead-magnet sign-ups | Google Form submissions | Google Forms/Sheets | 100+ |
| Conversion | Business enquiries / audit requests | Service form + WhatsApp enquiries | Google Sheets lead log | 6-10 |
| Advocacy | Branded intern posts | LinkedIn posts using #VedaTechnologyIntern | LinkedIn hashtag search | 50+ |

Review rhythm: quick check every Monday, mid-campaign review on Day 15, final report on Day 30.
