# 08 · LinkedIn Content Plan

**Goal:** Build Veda Technology into the most trusted name for free, project-based virtual internships and a credible small-business IT partner.

**Cadence:** 4-5 posts per week on the Company Page (Mon-Fri, 9-11 AM or 6-8 PM IST); founder/mentors reshare with a personal comment.

## Content mix

| Format | Share |
|---|---|
| PDF carousels (roadmaps, checklists) | 35% |
| Intern spotlights & project showcases | 25% |
| Text + image insight posts | 20% |
| Polls & questions | 10% |
| Service / process posts | 10% |

## Tactics

- Intern advocacy: every intern already shares task posts on LinkedIn - give them a template, ask them to tag the Company Page and use #VedaTechnologyIntern and #BuildYourProof.
- Weekly 'Intern Spotlight' that reshares the best intern post with mentor comments.
- Optimise the Company Page: navy/orange banner, tagline 'Internships that prove output', About section with tracks and services, Apply button.
- Reply to every comment within 24 hours; use comment keywords (e.g. 'ROADMAP') to start DM conversations.
- Hashtags (3-5 per post): #VedaTechnology #BuildYourProof #VirtualInternship #Internship2026 + track tag (#WebDevelopment, #DataScience...).

## Intern post template

> Day [X] of my [Track] internship at @Veda Technology - this week I built [project] using [tools]. The biggest thing I learned: [insight]. Mentor feedback helped me [improvement]. #VedaTechnologyIntern #BuildYourProof #[Track]
