# 09 · Instagram Content Plan

**Goal:** Reach students early, make Veda Technology memorable, and drive link-in-bio clicks to the Apply page.

**Cadence:** 5 feed posts per week (3 Reels + 2 carousels), 3-5 stories daily; best times 12-2 PM and 7-10 PM IST.

## Content mix

| Format | Share |
|---|---|
| Reels (tips, myths, 'day in the life') | 60% |
| Carousels (roadmaps, comparisons) | 40% |

## Tactics

- Bio: 'Free virtual internships + IT services | Level 1-3 | Verified certificates' with a link page (Apply, Tracks, Free Kit, Services).
- Story highlights: Apply, Tracks, Certificates, Reviews, Services, FAQ.
- Navy backgrounds with orange highlight text and one consistent Canva template per format.
- Hook in the first 2 seconds; captions with one CTA; on-screen text for silent viewing.
- Weekly story Q&A and polls; collaborate with student creators/campus ambassadors on reels.

## Sample Reel script - '5 red flags of a fake internship'

- **0-2s hook:** 'Paid ₹2,000 for an internship? Watch this.'
- **2-20s:** 5 red flags on navy cards - registration fee, no tasks, no mentor, no verification ID, certificate in one day.
- **20-27s:** 'A real internship has tasks, reviews and a verifiable certificate.'
- **End card:** 'Save this. Share it with your batch.'
