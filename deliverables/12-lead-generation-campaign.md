# 12 · Lead-Generation Campaign

**Campaign:** #BuildYourProof - 30-day organic lead-generation campaign

**Budget:** Rs 0 paid media. Organic channels, owned website, free tools (Canva, Google Forms/Sheets, GA4 concepts, Search Console concepts).

## Funnel A - Students & Graduates

**Lead magnet:** Free 'Fresher Project Kit' (10 resume-ready project ideas mapped to Veda tracks + a one-page resume template) and a free live session 'From Zero to Internship'.

| Stage | Action |
|---|---|
| Attract | Reels, carousels and LinkedIn posts on career and safety topics; intern posts with #VedaTechnologyIntern. |
| Capture | Link in bio / post CTA -> Google Form (name, email, college, year, track of interest) -> kit delivered by email. |
| Nurture | 3 follow-up emails/WhatsApp messages over 7 days: track guide -> intern showcase -> batch deadline. |
| Convert | Apply page with UTM tracking; applicants tagged by source in Google Sheets. |
| Advocate | Interns post completed tasks on LinkedIn using the template - feeding new top-of-funnel reach. |

## Funnel B - Small Businesses & Startups

**Lead magnet:** Free '10-Point Website Health Check' - a short checklist review of the business's site or Google Business Profile.

| Stage | Action |
|---|---|
| Attract | Facebook/LinkedIn tip posts, local business groups, Google Business Profile posts. |
| Capture | Short form: business name, website (if any), service needed, phone/WhatsApp. |
| Nurture | Deliver the health-check summary within 48 hours with 3 clear fixes. |
| Convert | Offer a call and a scoped quote for Web, App, Cloud, Data or Digital Marketing work. |
| Advocate | Ask satisfied clients for a Google review and permission for a short case study. |

## Tracking

All links use UTM parameters (utm_source=instagram|linkedin|facebook, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=buildyourproof). Leads are logged in a shared Google Sheet with source, date, segment and status (New -> Contacted -> Applied/Quoted -> Converted).
